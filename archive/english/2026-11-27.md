# 英语每日一课 · 2026-11-27（周五）

## 今日短文：Planning a Weekend Trip（计划周末旅行）

Tomorrow is Saturday, and my friend and I are going to take a short trip to a small town near the city. We have never been there before, so we are very excited. We have just booked a small hotel near the lake. It is clean and quiet, and it is not expensive. In the morning, we will walk around the old streets and take some photos. At noon, we will try the local food. I have heard that the noodles there are very delicious. In the afternoon, we will ride bikes by the lake and watch the sunset. It will be a relaxing weekend. I have already packed my bag, and I can't wait to go!

**中文翻译：** 明天是星期六，我和朋友要去城边的一个小镇短途旅行。我们以前从没去过那里，所以都很兴奋。我们刚订了一家湖边的小旅馆，又干净又安静，价格也不贵。早上，我们会在老街上逛逛、拍些照片；中午尝尝当地美食，听说那里的面条特别好吃；下午沿着湖边骑车、看日落。这会是一个轻松的周末。我已经收拾好了行李，迫不及待要出发了！

## 重点词汇（5 个）

- **trip** /trɪp/ 旅行；短途旅行 — We are going to take a short trip to a small town.
- **excited** /ɪkˈsaɪtɪd/ 兴奋的 — We have never been there before, so we are very excited.
- **book** /bʊk/ 预订 — We have just booked a small hotel near the lake.
- **local** /ˈləʊkl/ 当地的 — We will try the local food.
- **relaxing** /rɪˈlæksɪŋ/ 令人放松的 — It will be a relaxing weekend.

今天只背这 5 个，10 个封顶，别贪多。

## 今日语法点：现在完成时 have/has + 过去分词

说“已经做过某事 / 从没做过某事”，用 have/has + 动词过去分词。

- I have never been to the mountains.（我从没去过山里。）
- I have just booked a hotel near the lake.（我刚订了湖边的一家旅馆。）
- Have you ever tried the local food?（你尝过当地美食吗？）
