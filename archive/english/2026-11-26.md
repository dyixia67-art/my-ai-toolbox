# 英语每日一课 · 2026-11-26（周四）

## 今日短文：A Rainy Day at Home（下雨天，在家）

This morning, I woke up to the sound of rain on the window. It was a cold and rainy day, so I decided to stay at home. I made a cup of hot tea and sat by the window to read a book. The rain fell softly, and the room felt warm and quiet. After lunch, I watched a short film and called my friend to talk about our weekend plan. If the weather is fine tomorrow, we will go for a walk in the park. If it rains again, we will stay at home and cook together. A rainy day is not boring at all. It is a good time to slow down and rest.

**中文翻译：** 今天早上，我是被窗户上的雨声吵醒的。外面又冷又下雨，我决定待在家里。我泡了一杯热茶，坐在窗边看书。雨轻轻地下着，屋里又暖和又安静。午饭后，我看了一部短片，还给朋友打电话聊了聊周末的计划。如果明天天气好，我们就去公园散步；如果还下雨，我们就待在家里一起做饭。下雨天一点也不无聊——正是放慢脚步、好好休息的时候。

## 重点词汇（5 个）

- **sound** /saʊnd/ 声音 — I woke up to the sound of rain on the window.
- **rainy** /ˈreɪni/ 下雨的；多雨的 — It was a cold and rainy day.
- **softly** /ˈsɔːftli/ 轻轻地；柔和地 — The rain fell softly.
- **weekend** /ˈwiːkend/ 周末 — We talked about our weekend plan.
- **slow down** 放慢脚步；慢下来 — It is a good time to slow down and rest.

今天只背这 5 个，10 个封顶，别贪多。

## 今日语法点：If 条件句（如果……就……）

说“如果……，就……”，用 If + 一般现在时，主句用 will + 动词原形。

- If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home.（如果明天下雨，我就待在家里。）
- If the weather is fine, we will go for a walk.（如果天气好，我们就去散步。）
- If you are free, we will cook dinner together.（如果你有空，我们就一起做晚饭。）
