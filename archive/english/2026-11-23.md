## 今日短文：Cooking a Simple Dinner at Home（在家做一顿简单的晚饭）

Last night, I cooked a simple dinner at home. First, I washed some vegetables and cut them into small pieces. Then, I put some rice in the pot and turned on the rice cooker. After that, I fried two eggs with tomatoes. It was easy and quick. The kitchen smelled wonderful. At seven o'clock, I sat down at the table and enjoyed my dinner. It was not a big meal, but it was warm and healthy. Cooking at home is cheaper than eating out. It also makes me feel relaxed after a long day. Tomorrow, I want to try a new dish: vegetable soup.

**中文翻译：** 昨晚，我在家做了一顿简单的晚饭。首先，我洗了一些蔬菜，切成小块。然后，我把米放进锅里，打开电饭煲。接着，我用西红柿炒了两个鸡蛋。简单又快。厨房里香气四溢。七点，我坐到桌边享用晚饭。这顿饭不丰盛，但很暖和、很健康。在家做饭比出去吃便宜。忙碌一天后，做饭也让我感到放松。明天，我想试一道新菜：蔬菜汤。

## 重点词汇（5 个）

- **cut ... into pieces** /kʌt ... ˈɪntə ˈpiːsɪz/ 把……切成块 — I cut the vegetables into small pieces.
- **fry** /fraɪ/ 油煎；炒 — I fried two eggs with tomatoes.
- **smell** /smel/ 闻起来（有……气味） — The kitchen smelled wonderful.
- **relaxed** /rɪˈlækst/ 放松的 — Cooking makes me feel relaxed.
- **healthy** /ˈhelθi/ 健康的 — It was a warm and healthy dinner.

今天只背这 5 个，10 个封顶，别贪多。

## 今日语法点：It's + 形容词 + to do sth（做某事是……的）

想评价做一件事难不难、贵不贵，用 It's + 形容词 + to do sth，比只说 "It is easy" 更完整。

- It's easy to cook at home.（在家做饭很容易。）
- It's cheaper to cook at home than to eat out.（在家做饭比出去吃便宜。）
- It's relaxing to cook after a long day.（忙碌一天后做饭很放松。）
