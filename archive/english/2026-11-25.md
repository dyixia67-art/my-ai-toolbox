# 英语每日一课 · 2026-11-25（周三）

## 今日短文：A Walk in the Park（公园散步）

Yesterday afternoon, the weather was warm and sunny, so I decided to take a walk in the park near my home. I put on a light jacket and walked there slowly. In the park, some children were playing with a ball, and an old man was reading a book on a bench. I sat down under a big tree and listened to the birds singing. After a short rest, I walked around the lake and took some photos of the flowers. It was a quiet and relaxing afternoon. Tomorrow, I am going to walk in the park again. Walking outside makes me feel happy and full of energy.

**中文翻译：** 昨天下午天气暖和又晴朗，我决定去家附近的公园散步。我穿上一件轻薄的外套，慢慢走过去。公园里，一些孩子在玩球，一位老人正坐在长椅上看书。我在一棵大树下坐下，听鸟儿歌唱。短暂休息后，我绕着湖走了一圈，还拍了几张花的照片。这是一个安静又放松的下午。明天，我还要再去公园散步。户外散步让我感到开心、充满活力。

## 重点词汇（5 个）

- **decide** /dɪˈsaɪd/ 决定 — I decided to take a walk in the park.
- **jacket** /ˈdʒækɪt/ 外套；夹克 — I put on a light jacket.
- **bench** /bentʃ/ 长椅 — An old man was reading a book on a bench.
- **rest** /rest/ 休息 — After a short rest, I walked around the lake.
- **energy** /ˈenədʒi/ 精力；活力 — Walking outside makes me feel full of energy.

今天只背这 5 个，10 个封顶，别贪多。

## 今日语法点：be going to + 动词（说打算、说计划）

想说自己打算做什么，用 be going to + 动词原形，比 will 更口语、更常用。

- I'm going to walk in the park again tomorrow.（我明天还要再去公园散步。）
- She is going to visit her friend this weekend.（她这个周末要去看望朋友。）
- We are going to cook dinner at home tonight.（我们今晚打算在家做晚饭。）
