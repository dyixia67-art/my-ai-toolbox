## 今日短文：Asking for Directions（问路）

This morning, I walked to the old town to buy some fruit. I wanted to visit a small park, but I did not know the way. I stopped and asked an old man, "Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to Green Lake Park?" He smiled and said, "Go straight for five minutes, then turn left at the bank. The park is on your right." I thanked him and followed his words. After ten minutes, I saw the park. There were many trees and flowers, and people were walking their dogs. I sat on a bench and enjoyed the fresh air. Asking for directions is not hard. Most people are happy to help.

**中文翻译：** 今天早上，我去老城区买水果。想去一个小公园看看，但我不知道路。我停下来问一位老人：“请问，您能告诉我怎么去绿湖公园吗？”他笑着说：“直走五分钟，然后在银行那里左转。公园就在你右手边。”我谢了他，按他说的走。十分钟后，我看到了公园。里面有很多树和花，人们在遛狗。我坐在长椅上，享受着新鲜空气。问路并不难，大多数人都乐意帮忙。

## 重点词汇（5 个）

- **directions** /daɪˈrekʃnz/ 方向；问路 — Excuse me, could you tell me the directions to the park?
- **straight** /streɪt/ 直走；一直 — Go straight for five minutes.
- **turn** /tɜːrn/ 转弯 — Turn left at the bank.
- **bench** /bentʃ/ 长椅 — I sat on a bench and enjoyed the fresh air.
- **fresh** /freʃ/ 新鲜的 — I enjoyed the fresh air in the park.

今天只背这 5 个，10 个封顶，别贪多。

## 今日语法点：Could you tell me how to get to ... ?（问路句型）

想问路时说 Could you tell me how to get to + 地点，比直接问 "Where is ...?" 更礼貌。

- Could you tell me how to get to Green Lake Park?（你能告诉我怎么去绿湖公园吗？）
- Could you tell me how to get to the train station?（你能告诉我怎么去火车站吗？）
- Could you tell me how to get to the nearest hospital?（你能告诉我怎么去最近的医院吗？）

## Speak 实战任务

跟 AI 聊聊你在一个新地方问路：你要去一个公园/餐厅/地铁站，至少用 3 次 "Could you tell me how to get to ... ?"，再问一句 "How long does it take?"（要走多久？）。
