# 英语每日一课 · 2026-11-20（周五）

## 今日短文：Ordering Dinner at a Restaurant（在餐厅点餐）

Last night, my friend and I went to a small restaurant for dinner. We sat by the window and looked at the menu. The waiter came and asked, "Are you ready to order?" I said, "Yes, could I have the tomato beef noodles, please?" My friend ordered chicken soup and a salad. We also asked for two cups of green tea. The food came quickly. The noodles were hot and tasty, and the soup was just right. We talked and ate for about an hour. When we finished, I asked, "Could we have the bill, please?" The waiter smiled and brought it. Dinner cost us sixty yuan. We will go back next week.

**中文翻译：** 昨晚，我和朋友去一家小餐厅吃晚饭。我们坐在窗边看菜单。服务员过来问：“准备好点餐了吗？”我说：“好的，我要一份番茄牛肉面，谢谢。”朋友点了鸡汤和一份沙拉。我们还要了两杯绿茶。菜上得很快。面又热又好吃，汤也刚刚好。我们边聊边吃，大概吃了一个小时。吃完后我问：“可以买单吗？”服务员笑着把账单拿来了。这顿晚饭花了我们六十元。下周我们还会再去。

## 重点词汇（5 个）

- **menu** /ˈmenjuː/ 菜单 — Please look at the menu first.
- **waiter** /ˈweɪtər/ 服务员 — The waiter brought us two cups of tea.
- **order** /ˈɔːrdər/ 点餐；点菜 — Are you ready to order?
- **tasty** /ˈteɪsti/ 好吃的；美味的 — The noodles are hot and tasty.
- **bill** /bɪl/ 账单 — Could we have the bill, please?

今天只背这 5 个，10 个封顶，别贪多。

## 今日语法点：Could I have ... ?（礼貌点餐 / 要东西）

想礼貌地要某样东西时说 Could I have + 东西，比直接说 "I want ..." 更客气。

- Could I have the tomato beef noodles, please?（我可以要一份番茄牛肉面吗？）
- Could I have a cup of green tea, please?（我可以要一杯绿茶吗？）
- Could we have the bill, please?（我们可以买单吗？）

## Speak 实战任务

跟 AI 聊聊你去餐厅点餐：你点了什么、多少钱、味道怎么样。至少用 3 次 "Could I have ... ?"。
