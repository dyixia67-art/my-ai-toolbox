# 英语每日一课 · 2026-11-24（周二）

## 今日短文：Shopping at the Supermarket（去超市买东西）

This morning, I went to the supermarket near my home. I needed to buy some vegetables, fruit, and milk. First, I took a shopping basket and walked to the vegetable area. The tomatoes looked fresh and red, so I bought four of them. Then I bought some apples and a bottle of milk. At the checkout, the cashier asked, "Do you need a bag?" I said yes and paid with my phone. The shopping took me about thirty minutes. It was quick and easy. Now my fridge is full again, and I can cook a nice dinner tonight.

**中文翻译：** 今天早上，我去了家附近的超市。我需要买一些蔬菜、水果和牛奶。首先，我拿了一个购物篮，走到蔬菜区。西红柿看起来又新鲜又红，于是我买了四个。接着我买了一些苹果和一瓶牛奶。结账时，收银员问：“需要袋子吗？”我说要，然后用手机付了款。购物大概花了三十分钟，又快又方便。现在我的冰箱又满了，今晚可以做一顿好吃的晚饭。

## 重点词汇（5 个）

- **supermarket** /ˈsuːpəˌmɑːrkɪt/ 超市 — There is a supermarket near my home.
- **basket** /ˈbɑːskɪt/ （购物）篮 — I took a shopping basket at the door.
- **fresh** /freʃ/ 新鲜的 — The tomatoes looked fresh and red.
- **checkout** /ˈtʃekaʊt/ 收银台；结账处 — At the checkout, I paid with my phone.
- **pay** /peɪ/ 付款（过去式 paid）— I paid with my phone.

今天只背这 5 个，10 个封顶，别贪多。

## 今日语法点：How many / How much（问数量）

问“多少”时，可数名词用 How many，不可数名词用 How much。

- How many tomatoes did you buy?（你买了几个西红柿？）
- How much milk do you need?（你需要多少牛奶？）
- How much time did the shopping take?（购物花了多长时间？）
