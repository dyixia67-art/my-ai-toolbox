# 英语每日一课 · 2026-11-19（周四）

## 今日短文：My First Day at the Gym（第一次去健身房）

Yesterday was my first day at the gym. I felt a little nervous, but I told myself to just show up. It takes me about twenty minutes to walk there. I started with a five-minute warm-up on the treadmill. Then I tried the leg press machine. I used the lightest weight, and I did three sets of twelve. My legs felt tired, but I was happy. I did not try the heavy weights. I finished with a short stretch and went home. Today my legs feel a little sore, and that is normal. I will go again on Friday.

**中文翻译：** 昨天是我去健身房的第一天。我有点紧张，但我告诉自己：先到场就行。走过去大概要 20 分钟。我先在跑步机上热身 5 分钟，然后试了腿举机。我用的最轻的重量，做了 3 组，每组 12 次。腿有点累，但我很开心。我没碰大重量。最后拉伸了一下就回家了。今天腿有点酸，这很正常。周五我还会再去。

## 重点词汇（4 个）

- **nervous** /ˈnɜːrvəs/ 紧张的 — I felt a little nervous before my first class.
- **equipment** /ɪˈkwɪpmənt/ 器械；设备 — The gym has a lot of new equipment.
- **routine** /ruːˈtiːn/ 日常安排；常规 — I want to build a morning routine.
- **sore** /sɔːr/ 酸痛的 — My legs feel sore today.

## 今日语法点：It takes sb + 时间 + to do sth

做某事花某人多长时间。

- It takes me twenty minutes to walk to the gym.（走去健身房要花我 20 分钟。）
- It takes her ten minutes to cook breakfast.（她做早饭要花 10 分钟。）
- It takes us about an hour to finish the homework.（我们完成作业大概要花一小时。）

## Speak 实战任务

跟 AI 聊聊你第一次去健身房：花多长时间到、练了什么、感觉怎么样。至少用 3 次 "It takes me ... to ..."。
