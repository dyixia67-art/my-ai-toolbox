# 英语每日一课 · 2026-11-28（周六）

## 今日短文：A Busy Day at Work（忙碌的工作日）

Yesterday was a very busy day at work. I arrived at the office at nine o'clock, and my desk was already full of papers. In the morning, I had two meetings and answered many emails. While I was having lunch with my coworker, my boss called me and asked me to finish a report before five o'clock. I worked quickly, but I also took a short break to drink a cup of coffee. At last, I finished the report at half past four. I was tired, but I felt happy, because I did something important today.

**中文翻译：** 昨天是工作上非常忙碌的一天。我九点到达办公室，桌上已经堆满了文件。上午我开了两个会，还回复了很多邮件。和同事吃午饭的时候，老板打来电话，让我在五点前完成一份报告。我工作得很快，但也抽空喝了杯咖啡、休息了一下。最后，我在四点半完成了报告。我很累，但也很开心，因为今天我完成了一件重要的事。

## 重点词汇（5 个）

- **busy** /ˈbɪzi/ 忙碌的 — Yesterday was a very busy day at work.
- **coworker** /ˈkəʊwɜːkə(r)/ 同事 — I had lunch with my coworker.
- **report** /rɪˈpɔːt/ 报告 — I need to finish this report before five o'clock.
- **break** /breɪk/ 休息；间歇 — Let's take a short break and drink some coffee.
- **tired** /ˈtaɪəd/ 疲倦的 — I was tired, but I felt happy.

今天只背这 5 个，10 个封顶，别贪多。

## 今日语法点：While + 过去进行时，主句用一般过去时

说“正在做某事的时候，突然发生了另一件事”，用 While + was/were + 动词-ing，主句用一般过去时。

- While I was walking to work, it started to rain.（我正走路去上班，突然下起雨来。）
- While she was cooking dinner, her phone rang.（她正做晚饭，电话响了。）
- While we were watching a movie, the baby woke up.（我们正看电影，宝宝醒了。）
